**Financial Foundation & Revenue Planning — Conversation with Claude
Nonprofit Financial Path**

- The founding phase almost always runs at a personal financial loss — normal and expected; for a fixed-income founder, the cost is opportunity cost and out-of-pocket expenses, not foregone salary
- Stage progression: Personal/pre-org → Fiscal Sponsorship → Small Grants → Independent 501(c)(3) (Years 2–3)
- Fiscal sponsors like Open Collective Foundation or Code for Science & Society are the right near-term move — donors get tax deductibility, CSC gets legitimacy without legal overhead; typical admin fee 5–10%
- Early grant targets: local community foundations (Skagit County), Administration for Community Living (ACL), Area Agencies on Aging innovation funds
- Founder compensation is last and modest; realistic horizon for $12K–$24K/year is Year 2–3, contingent on grant success
- Before accepting any earned income: consult a Social Security benefits counselor (often free through senior centers or AAAs) — rules are navigable but nuanced

**Revenue Streams — Recommended**

- Consulting/advising to care organizations — $75–$150/hr, synergistic with CSC mission
- Writing and content — trade publications, potential Substack; builds CSC credibility simultaneously
- CSC Certification workshops — early version possible before full platform; revenue flows to organization
- Grant writing for other local nonprofits — builds relationships and skills CSC needs anyway

**Revenue Streams — Considered & Rejected**

- Tech contracting: competes with CSC time, not synergistic
- Affiliate marketing: direct conflict of interest with mission independence
- Premium community membership: premature until community has demonstrated value
- Part-time retail/service work: not ruled out for cash gaps, but lower ROI than consulting

**Terminology Clarified**

- AAA = Area Agency on Aging (not American Automobile Association) — ~600 local/regional agencies under the Older Americans Act; Skagit County's network is a natural early CSC partner

**Key Priorities Confirmed** 

- No new debt / protect debt-free status ✓
- Build sustainable open source community ✓
- Modest personal financial improvement (a cruise someday) ✓
- Avoid conflicts of business interest
