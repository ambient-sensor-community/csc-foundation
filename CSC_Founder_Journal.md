### Session 2 — Opening Conversation
*May 2026*

- **Concept validated:** The website is unusually strong for an early-stage project — the framing, principles, and "Maria" story are doing real work. Core weakness is a thin call-to-action and no founder story on About Us.

### Session 6 — AI and CC
*May 2026*

- Reviewed CC's "AI and the Commons" and CC Signals pages; both address the extraction of shared knowledge by AI without consent or reciprocity
- Identified a strong parallel: CSC is doing for home sensor data what CC is doing for creative works — building the ethical governance layer that technology skipped
