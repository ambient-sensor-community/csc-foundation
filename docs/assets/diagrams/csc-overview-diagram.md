## Current review status

This is the current authoritative draft of the CSC overview diagram.

It is suitable for discussion, early outreach, and internal review.  
It has not been formally adopted as a final public-facing CSC asset.

## Key messages

- Simple non-camera sensors can notice changes in ordinary routines.
- Household data remains locally controlled.
- Trusted people receive a gentle suggestion to check in when something appears different.
- CSC provides a stable open platform.
- Families, caregivers, technologists, and local experts build and improve the community bazaar.
- The community has the keys.

## Open review questions

- Is the amount of text appropriate for an introductory handout?
- Should CSC also create a simpler companion diagram for very brief conversations?
- Are the terms `local home hub`, `local processing`, and `stable open platform` understandable to nontechnical readers?
- Should the diagram distinguish more explicitly between the resident, the care circle, and the broader CSC community?
