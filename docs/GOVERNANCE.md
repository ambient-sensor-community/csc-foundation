# Governance

**Last updated:** May 2026  
**Status:** Draft — founding board formation in progress

## Organizational Structure

The Caring Sensor Community is organized as a nonprofit. Current status: seeking fiscal sponsorship on a path to independent 501(c)(3).

**Key principle:** Governance decisions are made by people with lived experience of the problem — caregivers, family members, and practitioners — not primarily by technologists or investors.

---

## Founding Board

**Status:** In formation. See [`docs/BOARD.md`](BOARD.md) for candidate profiles and search strategy.

**Target composition:**
- Caregiver/care recipient voice (1–2 seats)
- Technology / open-source expertise (1 seat)
- Community health / aging services (1 seat)
- Legal / nonprofit governance (1 seat)
- Design / communications (1 seat)

The founding board serves until the first community election, targeted for Year 2.

## Open Source Governance

Code repositories follow a **maintainer model** during the early stage:

- The founding team reviews and merges contributions
- All decisions about the public API and plugin spec are documented with rationale
- Breaking changes require a 60-day notice period once the community has active contributors

As the community grows, governance will evolve toward a model with elected maintainers and a formal RFC (Request for Comments) process for significant changes.

---

## Transparency Commitments

- Founder journal entries are shared publicly in this repository
- Board meeting notes will be published within 30 days of each meeting
- Financial reports will be published annually
- All governance decisions will be documented with rationale, not just outcomes

---

## Conflicts of Interest

Board members and maintainers disclose:
- Financial interests in companies building related products
- Consulting or employment relationships with care technology companies
- Family relationships with other board members or major contributors

Disclosure does not require recusal; undisclosed conflicts require it.
