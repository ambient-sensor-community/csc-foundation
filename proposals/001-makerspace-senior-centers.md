Proposal 001 — Sensor Makerspace at Senior Centers
Submitted by: Mark Shea · May 2026 · Status: Dream
The idea:
Seek funding to establish dedicated Makerspaces for building open-source wireless sensors, co-located with local Senior Centers.
Why it fits CSC's mission:
Places the tools of sensor development inside the communities that most need them. Co-location with Senior Centers closes the loop — builders and beneficiaries share a roof. All outputs published under CC-BY-SA, keeping community-generated knowledge in the commons where it belongs.
What it would take:
Grant funding (aging-in-place, digital equity, or community technology funders); a pilot Senior Center partner willing to host; equipment, tools, and facilitation; and volunteer or paid maker mentors to run workshops.
Dream outcome:
A replicable model — "Sensor Makerspace in a Box" — that any community can stand up. A Senior Center in Skagit Valley becomes the first. Others follow. The people most affected by home sensing technology become its builders, not just its subjects
