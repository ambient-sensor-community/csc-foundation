# Founding Board

**Last updated:** May 2026  
**Status:** 🔄 In progress — profiles being developed, outreach not yet begun

## Why This Board Matters

The founding board sets the organizational culture, governance norms, and strategic direction for everything that follows. We are looking for people who bring lived experience, not just credentials — and who understand that this is a ten-year project, not a sprint.

---

## What We're Looking For

Board members who:
- Have personal or professional experience with aging-at-home care
- Believe in open-source and community-built approaches to hard problems
- Can commit meaningful time during the formation stage (estimated 4–6 hours/month)
- Bring a specific expertise the founding team lacks
- Are willing to disagree and say so clearly
