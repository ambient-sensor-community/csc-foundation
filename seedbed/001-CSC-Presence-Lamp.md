# CSC Presence Lamp

**Type:** Seed
**Status:** Captured
**Submitted by:** Mark Shea
**Date:** June 2026

## The idea or question

Explore a modern CSC version of the paired presence lamp: a humane relationship device that allows two households to share a simple, temporary visual signal of presence.

A lamp in one home could glow gently when a participating person touches their lamp or moves nearby. The paired lamp in another home would glow for a limited period and then fade.

The device should communicate something small and legible:

> I am here. Something familiar happened. You may think of me.

It should not create a surveillance feed.

## Why it may fit CSC

The presence lamp expresses several core CSC ideas in a form that is easy to understand:

* modest technology can support relationships;
* sensing does not need to become surveillance;
* household data can be interpreted locally;
* only a minimal, consented signal needs to leave the home;
* technology can offer reassurance without pretending to eliminate risk.

## What appears promising

The lamp could serve as:

* a simple early prototype;
* a demonstration object for explaining CSC;
* a teaching tool for local-first design;
* a possible optional output from a future CSC household system.

A first version could be deliberately simple: a touch button, a pause switch, a soft light, and a paired connection.

A later version could optionally use a nearby motion sensor or locally interpreted routine signal.

## Significant assumptions

* A small visual signal may provide meaningful reassurance.
* Participants may prefer an ambient household object over a phone notification.
* Local interpretation and minimal sharing may reduce privacy concerns.
* A gentle signal can remain useful without becoming an expectation of constant availability.

## Risks, tensions, or unresolved questions

* A missing glow could cause unnecessary worry.
* Even simple presence signals may feel intrusive if consent is unclear.
* The device could become a disguised activity tracker if raw sensor data is shared.
* The best first prototype may be voluntary touch, nearby motion, or both.
* The lamp may need an obvious pause control and a clear way to communicate that sharing is disabled.
